Walking and cycling safety in Melbourne

R, QGIS · Independent project · 2026

Using 142,875 Victorian crash records, I found that half of the pedestrian and cyclist crashes from 2021 to 2025 happened in just 6% of metro Melbourne, and that pedestrians hit on 80+ km/h roads are far more likely to be killed or seriously hurt than on 40 km/h roads (65% against 41%).

Drugstore access in Thailand

ArcGIS · Published paper, co-author · 2023

A published GIS study I co-authored: only 199 of 511 villages near Bangkok could reach a pharmacy in the medicine pick-up scheme, and 22 well-placed new pharmacies would bring that to 417.

ESG and financial analytics, Asia-Pacific

Tableau · Curtin University · 2026

Three linked Tableau dashboards on 8 Asia-Pacific markets, 2010 to 2024. ESG score explains only about 2% of the difference in profit between firms, so a high rating alone is a poor guide to profitability.

Credit risk: what the loan data shows

R · Curtin University, group of three · 2025

With two classmates, I cleaned a 32,581-row loan dataset and found that about one loan in five had defaulted. A logistic regression model then predicted default with an AUC of 0.85 on unseen test data.

Australian housing price regression

Five regression models of quarterly house price growth in five Australian states. The COVID-19 period added about 2.6 percentage points to quarterly growth, while election years made no difference.
