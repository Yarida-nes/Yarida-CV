Credit risk: what the loan data shows

With two classmates, I cleaned 32,581 loan records down to 28,578 and found that one loan in five had defaulted. A regression on income explained about a third of the variation.

Five regression models of quarterly house price growth in five Australian cities. The model’s explanatory power rose from 5% to 24% as variables were added, and COVID-19 had the largest single effect.
